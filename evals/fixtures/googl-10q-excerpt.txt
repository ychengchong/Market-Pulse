Item 2. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
Results of Operations
Google Search & other revenues increased $6.8 billion and $25.6 billion from the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025 to the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. The overall growth was driven by interrelated factors including increases in search queries resulting from growth in user adoption and usage on mobile devices; growth in advertiser spending; and improvements we have made in ad formats and delivery.
